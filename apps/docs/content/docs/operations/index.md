This section covers running {{brand}} on your computer, and the parts a person operates for others. Most readers need only the
first few pages. The [runbook library](/docs/operations/runbooks) holds the detailed runbook for every part of the product.

## On your computer

- [Running the daemon](/docs/operations/running-the-daemon): the process that owns your documents, its private folder and how to stop it
- [Desktop app](/docs/operations/desktop-app): the desktop shell, and what it does and does not do yet
- [VS Code extension](/docs/operations/vscode-extension): the editor for `.vellum` files in VS Code, Cursor and Windsurf
- [Backups and recovery](/docs/operations/backups-and-recovery): what to keep, and what the recovery copies are for
- [Security and privacy](/docs/operations/security-and-privacy): what stays on your computer, what leaves it, and the checks behind the claims

## For the people who run the services

- [Accounts and Pro](/docs/operations/accounts-and-pro): device sign-in, Pro entitlements and the account service
- [Sync and self-hosting](/docs/operations/sync-and-self-hosting): the room service that carries live collaboration

## Releases

- [Updates and releases](/docs/operations/updates-and-releases): what a release needs, and what is still open
